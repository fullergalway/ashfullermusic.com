## Rights

The safest images for the site are her own gallery, the press photo (credit Leona Gilmore), and her official single art. Video frames have on-screen captions. Say that if you use one.

## Who she is

Aisling Fuller performs as Ash Fuller. She is an Irish singer-songwriter from Renmore, Galway. She is a Gaeilgeoir. Press in 2025 described her as 23. Her own page says she finds it easier to write feelings than to say them. Her page also says she sold out her first headline show at the Róisín Dubh in Galway.

She is not the composer Ash Fure, the CCM artist Ben Fuller, the pianist Asher Fulero, or the Las Vegas singer Ashley Fuller.

## How she looks

Young woman. Fair skin. Dark brown hair, worn long, in a braid, or in a bun. Small gold hoop earrings. She often has a dark Cort cutaway acoustic guitar. A common jacket is a navy and white varsity with the words INCOMING CALL on the chest. Pink shoes appear in several busking photos.

## Press and interviews to reopen later

- Galway Pulse, 27 October 2025. First song in Irish, “Inis Dom”. Photo credit Leona Gilmore. Photo 10.
- Connacht Tribune, 4 August 2025. Busking in Galway. Page later returned HTTP 403.
- Connacht Tribune, 26 January 2026. Piece on the debut single with Valter Romulo. Page later returned HTTP 403.
- Galway Bay FM. She spoke with John Morley in Irish and sang “Inis Dom”. She also spoke with Garry Curran on Plugged-In about “Sunday Mourning”, around 7 August 2025.

“Inis Dom” means “Tell Me”. She has said it was the second song she wrote in Irish, and that it is about having a bit of fun. She started to write in Irish in 2024. “Sunday Mourning” (June 2025) was written about a year earlier, in a hard time in a relationship. The sound is upbeat. The lyric is sad. She has named Taylor Swift as her biggest inspiration. She has said she fine-tunes a song and sits with it before release. Newer singles carry the label credit Aisling Records (℗ 2026).
